01

Nearstep

Count your no-contact days after a breakup, and take a pause before reaching out. Free to start, with no account.

Get Nearstep on the App Store

About this app

Nearstep 2.2 is available on the App Store. Each chosen contact restarts the current count; necessary contact does not. History and earned skies stay. Nearstep Plus is an optional one-time purchase, with no subscription. Android is not publicly available yet.

Nearstep is not therapy, medical care or emergency support. Explore Nearstep, support and privacy.

Personal health historyAvailable on iOS
02

Folumi

Your GLP-1 history, organised. Record injections, weight and notes, and review the schedule you entered. No account required.

Get Folumi on the App Store

About this app

Folumi 0.1 is available free on the App Store. Records stay on your device; there is no cloud sync or automatic recovery. Android is not publicly available yet.

Folumi does not guide dosing or replace care from a health professional. Explore Folumi, support and privacy.
